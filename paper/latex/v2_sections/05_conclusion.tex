\section{Conclusion and Limitations}
\label{sec:concl}

We formalized video-keyframe-grounded interleaved image-text generation, a task in which a system answers an instruction about a video with text and newly generated images that remain traceable to explicitly localized keyframes. Our framework couples an Understand Agent and a Generation Agent through a shared multimodal memory (Section~\ref{sec:method}), and VKIG-Bench (Section~\ref{sec:bench}) scores keyframe consistency and spatio-temporal localization alongside standard text and image measures. A temporal-oracle study identified keyframe timing as the dominant bottleneck of the pipeline, and the resulting two-stage selector raises joint localization accuracy to 0.567 on the HC-STVG~v2 evaluation subset, exceeding Zero-Shot STVG \citep{zeroshotstvg2509} on identical clips once both outputs are bridged to our joint keyframe metric, without any parameter training (Section~\ref{sec:exp}). On the generation side, our faithfulness protocol shows that instruction design and sequential editing control faithfulness in regimes where automatic metrics lose discriminative power, and a unified-model control illustrates what is lost when explicit localization is absent. The prototype runs end to end on a single consumer GPU. We leave composite anchoring that combines pixel identity, geometric layout, and free style text, fuller unified-model comparisons with BAGEL \citep{bagel2505} and Show-o2 \citep{showo22506}, and an independent second annotator to future work.

Several limitations qualify these results. On the localization side, temporal keyframe selection is only partially solved. Before the two-stage selector, temporal recall was 0.26 on V-STaR and 0.52 to 0.56 on HC-STVG, which capped joint localization at 0.23 and 0.42 to 0.44 respectively, even though spatial IoU reached 0.72 and 0.66 once the selected moment was correct. The selector lifts HC-STVG temporal recall to 0.720 and joint acc@0.3 to 0.567 on the 282-clip common subset, yet a gap of roughly 0.21 to the temporal oracle remains, and the coarse MLLM windowing stage adds one call per clip, measured at 1.46~s. The grid-density sweep is complete and follows an inverted U that peaks at $g=20$, so densifying the coarse stage cannot close the gap on its own; iterative window refinement, audio and subtitle cues, and a learned selector are natural next steps, and the two-stage result has not yet been re-verified on V-STaR. Grounding is comparatively mature in our runs: Qwen3-VL native grounding surpasses Grounding-DINO-base on the same-keyframe diagnostic spatial IoU (0.323 vs.\ 0.292) though not on the joint metric (0.22 vs.\ 0.24), and stronger detectors remain future work.

For generation and its evaluation, the human study is small and rests on a single author annotator. The baseline round ($n=40$), the matched A/B probe ($n=10$), the three-tier dual-axis ratings ($n=30$), and the best-tier round ($n=40$) carry no inter-annotator agreement, and the MLLM judge is validated only against those same labels, so independent multi-annotator re-labeling and a held-out judge validation are required before camera-ready or any large-scale automated use. The faithfulness and artistry axes compose only partway. Sequential two-pass editing breaks the single-instruction plateau, which stood at 90\% faithful with poster-likeness 1.50 in the ten-pair probe and at 70\% with 1.78 at $n=40$, and reaches 100\% faithful at poster-likeness 1.90 on the same keyframes; the chain then saturates, as three- and four-pass variants add no further artistry while faithfulness stays between 80\% and 100\%. Parallel anchors preserve only the modality they encode: IP-Adapter conditioning fails moment fidelity at every scale tested (0/10), and ControlNet-depth keeps the composition skeleton and reaches 2.30 artistry yet loses appearance entirely (0/10). The residual gap is appearance-preserving stylization, and all frontier measurements use ten pairs per arm from one annotator. A qualitative observation points the same way: adding a strong no-text constraint to a demo prompt visibly crowded out content preservation in one sample, consistent with a zero-sum single-instruction budget, which is also why decoupling across calls helps. The end-to-end V-STaR runs additionally used the earlier SDXL-Turbo generator, whereas the faithfulness study used FLUX-Kontext throughout.

The benchmark evidence also has provenance and scale limits. All numbers come from development-scale subsets rather than full splits: V-STaR 150 clips, HC-STVG 200 to 1000 clips, and the 282-clip common subset for the selector comparison. The HC-STVG videos were obtained from a community mirror rather than the official package; we verified that the mirror's annotation schema and gold tubes match the official format, and a re-run on the official release is planned. The heuristic noun-phrase extractor yields an over-long grounding phrase in roughly 29\% of HC-STVG samples, which conservatively depresses the reported numbers. The external comparisons run so far are the bridged Zero-Shot STVG baseline \citep{zeroshotstvg2509}, whose full DSTH mode we could not reproduce from the official code (an issue has been filed), and the Janus-Pro-7B control \citep{januspro2501} at $n=20$. Scaling to the full 1901-clip HC-STVG test split, growing VKIG-Bench from roughly 300 samples toward one to two thousand, running the end-to-end pipeline with the full FLUX-Kontext generator, adding the remaining metrics VQAScore and FID (DreamSim, DINOv2, and LPIPS are already reported), and evaluating stronger unified-model baselines are left to future work. Some design transfers likewise await verification: our coverage objective is an instantiation of AKS \citep{aks2502} rather than its published form, the SVO extraction is a transfer of GROVE \citep{grove2503}, and several 2026 preprint references and the M2RAG \citep{m2rag2411} metric set must be re-checked against source repositories before camera-ready.

Finally, the scope of the verified system is narrower than the full design. The reported runs execute the localization, grounding, and generation backbone; the shared-RAG store, the two feedback loops, and the multi-image interleaved layout are specified components that were not activated in any reported run, and no reported number depends on them. Domain coverage is limited to street-scene V-STaR \citep{vstar2503} and film-clip HC-STVG; short drama, the motivating application domain, remains at the stage of qualitative demonstrations. Our faithfulness notion is semantic rather than forensic: the protocol measures whether subject, scene, and composition match the source moment, and generative enhancement cannot recover identity-level detail absent from low-resolution sources, an information-theoretically ill-posed goal. Identity-critical scenarios therefore receive the localization output and the original frame only, and generated imagery is never treated as evidence; multi-frame super-resolution, which can recover genuine sub-pixel information, is orthogonal future work.
